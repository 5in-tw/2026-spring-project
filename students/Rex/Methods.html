Methods

Methodology Section

Study Design
First, we watched the documentary Twelve Nights to understand the topic and talked to a professional to get more information. Then, we visited an animal shelter. We used the fishbone and 5 Whys to choose a specific topic and find the actionable point. Finally, we did research online and worked with our teacher to write the research question.

1) Initial observation of the situation/subject
- Watch the documentary on Twelve Nights
2) Getting information so that you can design the survey accurately
1. Getting information from professional people
2. Going to the shelter
3) Find research topic
1. Fish bone
2. 5 whys
3. Find an action point
4) Come up with a research question
1. Do online research
2. Putting all the questions together
3. Discussing questions with the instructor
4. Revise the question with the instructor's advice

Participants
The participants were the people whom we asked in the survey. We made sure to collect answers from at least 50 people to have enough information for our study.

- People who participated in the survey
- 50 people minimum

Materials/Tools
We used a survey with a list of questions to collect data. We also used pens, paper, and a large poster to show the questions and choices to the participants.

- Survey (design for collecting data)
- Question list
- Stationery
- Poster for questions and choices

Procedure
First, we divide into groups and give everyone a job. The Speaker talked to people, the Recorder wrote the answers, and the Helper held the posters. We went to a public place and introduced our project. We talked to people face-to-face to get data. At the end, we asked them for their Gmail addresses if they wanted to see the results.

1) Form groups
2) Assign jobs for the survey
- Speaker: Catch people and tell them what we are doing. Tell them the question
- Recorder: Write down the answer
- Helper: Hold the things to show the options of the questions
3) Go to the place for the survey
4) Conducting the survey
1. Introduce ourselves and our project
2. Collecting data with face-to-face means
3. Ask if they want results
- ask for their Gmail

Special Considerations
We followed some rules to make sure we’re doing in a right way. We spoke loudly, stayed in one spot, and did not change our jobs or our introduction. We were very respectful: we did not block people’s way or chase them to make sure our survey was fair.

- Giving the survey
- Speak loudly
- Find a good place
- don’t ask more than one person at the same time
- consistent
- don’t change the position you’re standing
- don’t change the introduction
- don’t switch jobs
- be respectful
- don’t say bad words
- don’t sit down
- don’t chase the people
- don’t block the way
- avoid bias

Data Analysis
To understand our data, we put all the answers together and organized them. We made charts so the information was easy to see. And, we described the results for our specific area and wrote a conclusion about what we learned.
