I think a good person is someone who thinks about how their actions affect other people. Being good is not only about being nice. It means helping others, respecting their feelings, and not hurting them for selfish reasons.

A good person thinks about how their actions affect other people. This is important because even a small action can hurt someone if you only think about yourself. For example, if someone spreads a rumor as a joke, they may think it is funny, but it can embarrass another person and make other people think badly of them.

A good person does not hurt others for selfish reasons. If someone causes pain just to get attention, power, or something they want, that is selfish. For example, bullying is evil because a bully may want to feel powerful, but they get that feeling by making another person feel hurt, afraid, and alone.

A good person respects other people’s feelings. Other people’s feelings matter, and ignoring them can lead to cruel actions. For example, laughing at someone’s weakness may seem like a joke, but it can really hurt their feelings and make that person feel ashamed.

Being good means thinking about others, respecting their feelings, and not hurting them for selfish reasons. This shows that we should think not only about what we want, but also about how our actions affect other people. We should try to think about others before we act.
