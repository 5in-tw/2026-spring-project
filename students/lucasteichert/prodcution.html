Animatic

Script

Beginning

The dog spends every day happily with its owner. Every morning, they walk around the school together, play outside on the field, and sit near the benches during lunch. The owner pets the dog often, gives it treats, and laughs while playing fetch. The dog feels safe, loved, and always stays close to its owner because it trusts them completely. As time passes, the owner slowly begins acting differently. They seem more stressed and distracted and spend less time paying attention to the dog. Even though things feel different, the dog still follows the owner faithfully and believes everything is okay. One day after school, the owner brings the dog to a bench near the edge of campus. The owner gently pets the dog, ties the leash to the bench, and tells the dog to wait there. Thinking it is only temporary, the dog sits patiently and watches happily as the owner walks away, expecting them to come back soon like always.

Middle

At first, the dog waits patiently and excitedly. Students walk past while the dog keeps looking around for its owner. Every time footsteps approach, the dog stands up hopefully, thinking the owner has returned. As time passes, the school becomes quieter and the sun begins to set. The dog slowly becomes confused and nervous. Some students ignore the dog, while others look scared or uncomfortable around it. The dog tries pulling against the leash and following people, hoping someone will help, but it cannot go far because it is tied to the bench. As the school becomes emptier, the dog grows tired, hungry, and lonely.Eventually, the dog lies down alone near the bench, still waiting and hoping its owner will return.

Ending

The next day, a student notices the dog still tied to the bench in the same place. Instead of ignoring it, the student slowly approaches and sits beside the dog. The student gives the dog water and gently pets it. At first, the dog is nervous and unsure, but slowly begins to trust the student. For the first time since being abandoned, the dog feels safe again. The student unties the leash and walks away together with the dog, giving it a new home and a second chance at happiness. The film ends with the dog finally resting peacefully beside someone who truly cares for it.
