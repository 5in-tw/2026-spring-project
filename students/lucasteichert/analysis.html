Analysis

After we got the survey results, we compared them with our predictions. Some answers were close to what we thought, but some answers were different.

For Q1, we predicted that about 75% of people would choose No. The real result was 25 Yes and 25 No. This means both answers were 50%. Our prediction was not correct because more people answered Yes than we expected.

For Q2, we predicted that most people would choose not enough money. The real result was different. The biggest difficulty was safety / fear of dogs, with 16 people, or 32%. Not enough money only had 2 people, or 4%. This surprised us because money was not the biggest problem.

For Q3, we predicted that many people would choose dogs visiting public areas, such as parks or streets. This answer was popular, with 25 mentions, or 50%. But the top answer was dog cafes and restaurants, with 32 mentions, or 64%. So our prediction was partly correct, but it was not the top answer.

For Q4, we predicted that more people would choose pick-up service. The real result showed that public transport was the most popular, with 32 mentions, or 64%. Taxi was second, with 24 mentions, or 48%. Pick-up service only had 12 mentions, or 24%. This means our prediction was not correct.

For Q5, we predicted that most people would choose interaction and play. The real result was close. The most common answer was safe interaction / spending time with dogs, with 17 people, or 34%. This is similar to our prediction because both ideas are about spending time with dogs.

After comparing the results, we found that some predictions were close, but not all of them were correct. These results helped us understand the survey answers better.

conclusion


From this survey, we learned that people may want to meet dogs, but they also need to feel safe. Before the survey, we thought money would be the biggest problem. But the results showed that safety / fear of dogs was a bigger problem. This was different from our prediction.


We also learned that the place is important. Many people chose dog cafes, restaurants, schools, and public areas. This shows that people may like to meet dogs in places they already know or places that are easy to go to. If the dogs only stay in shelters, some people may not go there.

Transportation is also important. Many people chose public transport or taxi. This means the place should not be too hard to reach.

In conclusion, dogs can get closer to people if the meeting place is safe and easy to visit. People also need some information before they meet the dogs. If this can be done better, more people may notice the dogs and some dogs may have a better chance to be adopted.
