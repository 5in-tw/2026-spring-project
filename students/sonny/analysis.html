analysis

In question 1, we asked 51 people if they know any financial support for adopting a dog, and only one person clearly answered that they are aware of such support. For question 2, “Please name any financial support you know of for dog ownership in general,” the result was slightly better, with around 5 to 6 people able to give an answer. However, this number is still very small, showing that most people are not familiar with any financial assistance programs related to dog adoption.


For Question 3, which asked whether financial planning support would increase respondents’ willingness to adopt a dog, the majority of participants gave positive responses, such as “Agree” or “Yes 1/2.” This indicates that financial concerns are an important factor when people consider adopting a dog. Many potential adopters may worry about the long-term costs of pet ownership, including food, medical care, grooming, and other unexpected expenses. Even though many respondents may not be aware of the support programs that already exist, the results suggest that providing financial planning advice could make adoption seem more affordable and manageable. Therefore, offering financial guidance or financial assistance programs may encourage more people to adopt dogs from shelters and help increase overall adoption rates.


In question 4, opinions about increasing taxes on breeders and pet shops were more divided.

Most people disagree, with overall 33% disagreeing, and with 29.4%agreeing. Also 25% of respondents were neutral, with no opinion. This is important because these responses show that people have different ideas, some think it could help increase the number of dog adoptions.

In Question 5, respondents were asked whether better vet care would increase their willingness to adopt a dog.
Most respondents agreed or strongly agreed with this statement, showing strong support for improving animal healthcare services. Only a small number of people disagreed, indicating that vet care is an important consideration for potential adopters.
This is important because many people may be concerned about the health and medical costs of owning a dog. Better vet care can make adoption seem more reliable and less stressful. Overall, the results suggest that improving vet services could increase adoption willingness and help encourage more people to adopt dogs from shelters.
Overall, the data shows that awareness of financial support is very low, but people are generally more willing to adopt if financial assistance or better animal care systems are provided. However, opinions are more mixed when it comes to policy changes such as increasing taxes on breeders.


Conclusion:

The survey showed that most people do not know about financial support for dog adoption. However, many respondents said that financial planning support and better vet care would make them more willing to adopt a dog. Overall, providing more support and information may help increase dog adoption rates.
