Production journal

In this semester, we had two videos to film: a practice video and a final cut. In the group, my job was filming. During this process, I learned a lot about filming, including filming techniques and camera angles. I learned how different angles can create different effects and make a video more interesting. I also learned the importance of teamwork because everyone had to work together to finish the project. These experiences helped me improve my filming skills and taught me how to create better videos.
