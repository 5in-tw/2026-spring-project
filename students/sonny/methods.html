Methods

Study Design

This study started by watching the documentary Twelve Nights to learn about animal shelters and abandoned animals. Information was then collected by talking to professionals and visiting shelters. The group used fish bone and 5 Whys methods to find the research topic and action points. Finally, online research and discussions with the instructor helped create and revise the research question.

1.Initial observation of situation/subject

1.Watch the documentary of Twelve Nights

2.Getting information so that you can design the survey accurately

1.Getting information from professional people

2.Going to the shelter

3.Find research topic

1.Fish bone

2.5 whys

3.Find action point

4.Come up with research question

1.Do online research

2.Putting all the questions together

3.Discussing questions with instructor

4.Revise the question with instructor advice

Procedure

The class formed groups and assigned jobs such as speaker, recorder, and helper. The groups then went to the survey location and collected data through face-to-face surveys.

1.Form groups

2.Assign jobs for survey

1.Speaker: Catch people and tell them what we are doing. Tell them the question

2.Recorder: Write down the answer

3.Helper: Hold the things to show the options of the questions

3.Go to place for survey

Conducting the survey

1.Introduce ourselves and our project

2.Collecting data with face-to-face means

3.Ask the question before asking their age

4.Ask if they want results

Special Considerations

During the survey, the group spoke clearly, stayed respectful, avoided bias, and kept the survey process consistent.

Giving the survey

Speak loudly

Find a good place

don’t ask more than one person at the same time

Consistent

1.don’t change the introduction

2.don’t switch job

be respectful

don’t say bad word

don’t sit down

don’t chase the people
